${{ values.name }} deployed as a WebService ({{ .Release.Name }}).

The Crossplane composition owns the Deployment, Service, HTTPRoute and friends.

1. Check the WebService status (URL appears once it is exposed):
   kubectl get webservice {{ .Release.Name }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
     -o jsonpath='{.status.phase}{"\t"}{.status.url}{"\n"}'

2. Watch the composed resources reconcile:
   kubectl get deployment,service,httproute -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
     -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ .Release.Name }}
